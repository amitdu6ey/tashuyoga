About Tashu

A Journey Rooted in Tradition, Open to All

My name is Tashu — a certified yoga instructor, wellness advocate, and lifelong student of the ancient science of breath, movement, and awareness. What began as a personal search for stillness has grown into a calling: to guide hundreds of students toward strength, clarity, and inner peace.

Trained in Rishikesh and certified at the RYT-500 level, I bring together anatomical precision, traditional Hatha and Vinyasa lineage, and a deeply human approach to teaching. My classes are spaces where every body is welcome — regardless of age, flexibility, or experience.
