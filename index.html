About Tashu

A Journey Rooted in Tradition, Open to All

My name is Tashu, and yoga found me during a season of transformation. What started as a search for stillness became a lifelong dedication to the ancient science of breath, movement, and awareness.

I trained in Rishikesh, India — the yoga capital of the world — completing 500 hours of study under traditional Hatha and Vinyasa teachers. My classes blend anatomical precision with soulful guidance, making the practice accessible and deeply personal.
